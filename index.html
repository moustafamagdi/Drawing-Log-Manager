Controlled Decisions

Official drawing numbers are never hard-deleted or renumbered through the UI. Naming dimensions are fixed after allocation; use Duplicate to create a new controlled number. Lifecycle is handled by Active / Withdrawn / Cancelled. Stage can change without incrementing revision. Code-list entries are retired by setting them inactive rather than deleting them.
